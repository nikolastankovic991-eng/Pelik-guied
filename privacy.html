2. What information may we collect?

  • Business application details, such as business name, category, village, address, business contact details, website, description and optional photo URL.
  • Claim, correction and removal requests, including the requester's name, email address, optional phone number and message.
  • Information provided when contacting the project or using administrator functions.
  • Technical information that may be processed by website, hosting, security and infrastructure providers when pages are requested. The precise logs and retention periods depend on those providers' configurations.

3. Why do we use this information?

  • To review business listing applications and decide whether a listing may be published.
  • To assess requests to claim, correct or remove a listing and contact the requester.
  • To maintain the accuracy, security and operation of the directory.
  • To respond to enquiries and meet applicable legal obligations.

4. Legal basis

The legal basis depends on the particular request and the relationship with the person submitting it. Depending on the circumstances, processing may be necessary to take steps requested before entering into an arrangement, to pursue legitimate interests such as operating and protecting the directory, or to comply with a legal obligation. Where consent is relied upon, it will be requested separately and may be withdrawn. The controller must confirm and document the appropriate legal basis for each processing activity before launch.

5. Who may receive the information?

Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the website, database, hosting, security and delivery infrastructure, including providers such as Supabase, GitHub and relevant content-delivery services. Access should be limited to what is necessary. The controller must confirm the providers' contractual terms, data-processing arrangements, locations and any international-transfer safeguards before launch. We do not intend to sell personal information or use it for unrelated advertising.

6. Public business listings

Only listings approved for publication by the administrator are intended to appear in the public directory. Published business information may include business name, category, village, address, business contact details, website, description and approved photographs. Do not submit personal contact details for publication unless you are authorised to do so.

7. How long is information kept?

Information should be kept only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, taking account of unresolved requests, operational needs and legal obligations. Specific retention periods have not yet been approved and must be set by the controller before public launch. A documented schedule should cover rejected applications, completed requests, published listing records, security logs and backups.

8. Your data protection rights

Subject to the conditions and exceptions in applicable law, you may have the right to request access to your personal data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, or to object to certain processing. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. Contact the controller using the details above. The controller will respond within the time required by law.

You may also lodge a complaint with Malta's Information and Data Protection Commissioner (IDPC): idpc.org.mt. See the Commissioner's guidance on your rights.

9. Cookies and external links

This site may load third-party scripts or services needed for its functions. Their operation may involve technical data or cookies governed by the relevant provider's terms. External websites, including map and business websites, have their own privacy practices. A full cookie and third-party-resource review is still required before launch.

10. Security and changes

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. This notice may be updated as the service changes; the latest version and date will be published here.
